## How this was measured

A headless Chromium over CDP at 390, 768 and 1280. For each route: controls
without an accessible name, images without alt, duplicate ids, heading order,
fields without a label, positive tabindex, and tables without a header row.
Keyboard paths were driven with real key events through `Input.dispatchKeyEvent`,
not scripted `.focus()`, because a scripted focus does not fire what a real one
fires and reports a false negative on the hover card.

What came back clean: every control has a name, no duplicate ids, no positive
tabindex, one `h1` per route, every field is labelled, the drills are a real
fieldset per question with a live region for the mark, reduced motion is
honoured, and a cold boot is 11 requests to first contentful paint.
